Our story

Our story

From a sole proprietorship to a group of companies

Country Trading began in 1988 as a sole proprietorship. It became a private limited company to support its growth and now runs several business divisions with local and international partners.

Our purpose

We believe Ethiopians deserve the best affordable goods and services available globally.

Our ambition

To make Ethiopia not only a destination for quality products, but a source of them.
