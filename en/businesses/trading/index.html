Import & Distribution

Sourcing quality goods worldwide and distributing them across Ethiopia.

We import and distribute premium-quality goods in Ethiopia, working with local and international partners, including beverage and tobacco brands. Our main warehouse sits in the industrial area of Addis Ababa, less than ten kilometres from the airport and the main customs depot.
